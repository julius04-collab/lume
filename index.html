Ingredient spotlight

Niacinamide, the quiet multitasker.

Also known as vitamin B3, niacinamide is one of the most researched actives in skincare: gentle enough for daily use, effective enough to notice.

  • Refines the look of pores

    Helps regulate excess oil so pores appear smaller and skin looks smoother.

  • Evens out tone

    Helps fade the look of dark marks and dullness with consistent use.

  • Supports the skin barrier

    Helps skin make its own ceramides, so it holds on to moisture for longer.
